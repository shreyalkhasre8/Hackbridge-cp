HackBridge relational schema
Primary keys:
students.csv: (student_id)
colleges.csv: (college_id)
skills.csv: (skill_id)
student_skills.csv: (student_id, skill_id)
hackathons.csv: (hackathon_id)
hackathon_required_skills.csv: (hackathon_id, skill_id)
teams.csv: (team_id)
team_members.csv: (team_id, student_id)
mentors.csv: (mentor_id)
mentor_skills.csv: (mentor_id, skill_id)
team_mentor_mapping.csv: (team_id, mentor_id)
projects.csv: (project_id)
hackathon_participation.csv: (participation_id)
team_hackathon_compatibility.csv: (team_id, hackathon_id)

Foreign keys:
students.college_id -> colleges.college_id
student_skills.student_id -> students.student_id
student_skills.skill_id -> skills.skill_id
hackathon_required_skills.hackathon_id -> hackathons.hackathon_id
hackathon_required_skills.skill_id -> skills.skill_id
projects.student_id -> students.student_id
teams.leader_student_id -> students.student_id
teams.hackathon_id -> hackathons.hackathon_id
team_members.team_id -> teams.team_id
team_members.student_id -> students.student_id
hackathon_participation.student_id -> students.student_id
hackathon_participation.hackathon_id -> hackathons.hackathon_id
hackathon_participation.team_id -> teams.team_id
mentor_skills.mentor_id -> mentors.mentor_id
mentor_skills.skill_id -> skills.skill_id
team_mentor_mapping.team_id -> teams.team_id
team_mentor_mapping.mentor_id -> mentors.mentor_id
team_hackathon_compatibility.team_id -> teams.team_id
team_hackathon_compatibility.hackathon_id -> hackathons.hackathon_id

Additional constraints: (student_id,hackathon_id) is unique in participation. Each participation team belongs to that event and the student has Accepted membership. Leaders are Accepted members. Team size counts Accepted members only. Mentor mappings cover current teams only and honor mentor capacity. Historical rosters can recur across different dates. Candidate compatibility pairs are not registrations.
